    Inclusive Impact

    The Classroom Accessibility Divide

    Over 430 million people worldwide experience disabling hearing loss. In academic lecture halls, traditional methods fall short.

    ❌

    The Status Quo Problem

    • Cognitive fatigue: Lip-reading in large lecture halls is exhausting and captures less than 35% of spoken dialogue.
    • Zero context recovery: If a student looks down to write notes, they miss crucial technical explanations forever.
    • Sound blindness: Emergency sirens, door knocks, and teacher applause go unnoticed without visual acoustic cues.
    • Delayed study materials: Transcripts are rarely provided, leaving deaf students lagging behind peers during exams.
    ✅

    The SignAid Solution

    • Real-time zero-latency captions: Continuous streaming with clear interim feedback and teacher speaking indicators.
    • Instant "I Missed That" simplifications: On-demand AI breakdowns of the last 45 seconds prevent students from falling behind.
    • Web Audio sensory alerts: Sudden acoustic spikes flash visually on screen to keep students environmentally aware.
    • Automated Study Kits: Instant post-lecture flashcards, executive summaries, and comprehension quizzes ready immediately after class.
